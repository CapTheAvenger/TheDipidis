Post templates

Four templates at 1080 × 1350 for @thedipidis. Background, blossoms, logo, header and footer are the same ones the site uses for the images it generates itself — anyone arriving from Instagram recognises the site. Pick a source, adjust the text, download the image.

Numbers come from the site, not from typing. Everything stays editable.

Feature

English interface, dark mode. Phone (440 × 956) stands upright next to the bullets and stays readable; Desktop (2560 × 1440) lies across the top and shows the shape of a view rather than its content.

Number

No rate without its denominator. On an image travelling through Instagram nobody can look up what a percentage refers to. Two lines — a third is dropped silently. This was a one-line field until 04.09.2026; an <input> swallows every line break, and two sentences ran together in the image.

Ranking

Bars shows each value against the largest one — right for shares that add up to a field. Plain drops the bar, for numbers whose ratio says nothing (a 21.7 % Day-2 chance next to 17.1 % is not a bar). Table is the white board, right when a row carries two values.
